## Who are the contemporary Nordic artists to know?

Start with the survey, because nobody can hold the whole scene in their head. **Nordic Contemporary: Art from Denmark, Finland, Iceland, Norway and Sweden** (edited by Hossein Amirsadeghi and Sophie Braine, Thames and Hudson, 2015) gives you the lie of the land: profiles of roughly 150 artists, curators and galleries across all five nations, generously illustrated. It's the single best answer to who is doing what, and where. [On Amazon](https://www.amazon.co.uk/dp/0500970653).

## Contemporary Scandinavian artists: Eliasson, Andersson and Tal R

**Olafur Eliasson: Experience** (Phaidon, revised and expanded edition, 2022). The Icelandic-Danish artist who turned an artificial sun at Tate Modern into a national event. Nearly three decades of light, water, weather and glass gathered into one heavy, generous volume. If a single contemporary Nordic name has gone properly global, it's his. [On Amazon](https://www.amazon.co.uk/dp/1838665684).

**Mamma Andersson: The Lost Paradise** (David Zwirner Books, 2021). Sweden's finest living painter, with a new essay by Karl Ove Knausgård. Her interiors and landscapes are dreamlike and faintly unsettling, folk memory filtered through a muted palette. The colour reproductions here do the brushwork justice. [On Amazon](https://www.amazon.co.uk/dp/1644230569).

**Tal R**, by Martin Herbert (Lund Humphries, Contemporary Painters series, 2018). The first full study of the Copenhagen-based painter, whose work sits somewhere between symbol, memory and pure colour. A clear, well-argued way into one of Denmark's most distinctive voices. [On Amazon](https://www.amazon.co.uk/dp/1848222319).

## Modern Nordic art from Norway: Ida Ekblad

**Ida Ekblad: Melted Snow** (Lenz Press and Kunsthalle Zurich, 2022). Ekblad's first monograph, and a jolt of pure energy: a Norwegian painter and sculptor who works fast and loud, pulling in poetry, scrap metal and graffiti. Proof, if it were needed, that the Nordic scene is anything but quiet, and a useful corrective if you came to this part of the world expecting nothing but pale wood and restraint. [At Lenz Press](https://www.lenz.press/products/melted-snow).

Read a few of these together and the through-line appears: the same northern light and long winters that shaped the old masters still run through the work being made today. The photographers get their own instalment, [Nordic photography books](/article/nordic-photography-books), and they tell much the same story. Our own artists belong to it too, [Nordic illustrators and painters working now](/artists), and the [abstract prints](/category/abstract) sit naturally on the same shelf as these books. If one of them sends you looking for a piece to live with, the collection is a good place to wander.
