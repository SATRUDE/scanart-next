# Selected Mikko room scenes, 28 September 2026

The live Mikko artist and product pages still used older 848 × 1264 room scenes. This change carries three Mark-selected generated scenes into the shop. The original product artwork files and their warm framed-card renditions stay as the primary images. URF! remains on its current scene because explicit visual acceptance of test 27 was not found.

Each source was generated through the built-in ChatGPT image surface, with model identity unverified and artwork rendered within the room. All three source PNGs are 1122 × 1402 pixels. Shop AVIFs use quality 70, WebP twins quality 88; both carry XMP IPTC `DigitalSourceType=trainedAlgorithmicMedia`, descriptive title and description. Neither conversion crops or composites an image.
