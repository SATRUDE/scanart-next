# Go-live email: the link ask

Every new artist's go-live email carries this ask (T-0173, Mark, 7 October 2026). It is the wording Mark approved in T-0131, unchanged; only the greeting and sign-off are left to the rest of the email. Rules: their exact English artist page URL, no compliments on their work or photos, no em dashes, and it stays optional.

Mark writes and sends the go-live email himself. Staff may prepare a draft with this block in it, never send it.

```
A small favour: if you have a portfolio website or a links page, could you add a link to your page on Scandinavian Art? Here it is: https://www.scandinavianart.co.uk/artist/<slug>

Links from other sites are one of the main ways Google decides which pages to show in search, so every artist who links to their page helps people find the whole gallery, including your prints. It also gives anyone who finds you through your own site an easy way to buy a print.

Something as simple as "Prints available at Scandinavian Art" with the link in your footer, your shop page or your bio is perfect. No worries at all if it doesn't suit.
```

Then follow step 12 of `docs/add-an-artist.md`: a Today item two weeks later to check whether the link is there, and at most one gentle nudge if it is not.
